## NEW CONCEPTS FROM ICT (Not in Korabi's framework — research-validated)

These came up while researching the playlist content. **Evaluate each: should it be in your playbook?**

### A. IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm)
**What:** ICT's theory that price is delivered by an algorithm using 20/40/60-day lookback ranges.
**Impact on you:** This is THEORY, not directly tradeable. But it explains *why* the IRL/ERL cycle works.
**Decision:** Understand it, don't trade off it. Reference only.

### B. Quarterly Theory
**What:** Time is fractal. Each session = 4 quarters of 90 min. Each quarter = AMD (Accumulation/Manipulation/Distribution).
**Impact on you:** Could directly solve your "which model when" problem. The Manipulation quarter is where Rev setups form; the Distribution quarter is where Continuation setups form.
**Decision:** **HIGH POTENTIAL — recommend deep-dive.** Search for "Daye's Quarterly Theory ICT" specifically.

### C. Silver Bullet Strategy
**What:** A specific time-based ICT model using 3 one-hour windows (London 3-4am, NY AM 10-11am, NY PM 2-3pm).
**Setup:** Mark liquidity → wait for window → MSS → FVG entry → 1:3 R:R target.
**Win rate per research:** 55–65% (note: more conservative than your "85–90% Rev Model" claim).
**Impact on you:** This is essentially a time-filtered, structured version of your current setups. **Could become a 5th model** in your playbook, OR could replace MFD on non-news days.
**Decision:** **Research deeper. Likely valuable add.**

### D. Macro Times (20-min algorithmic windows)
- 09:50–10:10 ET (Silver Bullet AM)
- 10:50–11:10 ET
- 11:50–12:10 ET
- 13:10–13:50 ET
- 14:50–15:10 ET
- 15:15–15:45 ET

**Decision:** **Test in next 20 paper trades** — does taking only setups within these windows improve your win rate? Easy to measure.

### E. CISD (Change in State of Delivery)
**What:** A subtle HTF order flow shift that precedes a visible MSS on LTF. Used as advance warning.
**Impact on you:** Adds precision to "is this a real MSS or fakeout?" — your validation gap.
**Decision:** **High value. Add to L2 in Knowledge Graph.**

### F. MMXM (Market Maker eXternal Move)
**What:** Alternative framework for IRL → ERL movement. Very similar to your existing setup logic.
**Decision:** Cross-reference, but you don't need a duplicate model.

### G. CLS (Central Bank Liquidity Sweep)
