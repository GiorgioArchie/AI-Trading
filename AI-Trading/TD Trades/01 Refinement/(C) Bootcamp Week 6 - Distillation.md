## 2. Specific Rules, Numbers & Definitions (verbatim where it matters)

### The Two Models (and how they relate)
- **Reversal Model** = used when your **lower timeframe is the opposing draw** relative to a bullish HTF. You reverse the LTF back into HTF order flow. *"Reversal models when your lower timeframe is the opposing draw you reverse into the higher timeframe."*
- **Continuation Model** = used when the reversal **already happened / order flow is already aligned**; you re-enter to continue into the objective. *"Continuation models — add previous structure for draw."*
- Decision logic in practice: *"If the reversal isn't clear, which sometimes it really isn't… we are having difficulty identifying a reversal model, but we already reversed — what are we looking for now? Continuation."* → **When the reversal is messy/missed, default to continuation.**

### Reversal Model — step-by-step build (verbatim sequence)
1. **Fill an FVG** (lower-timeframe pullback into a larger HTF FVG).
2. **Displacement / shift above the previous structured high** (this IS the buy sequence / displacement — confirmed in Q&A: *"is the buy sequence the displacement? Yes."*).
3. **Buy sequence** confirms the shift.
4. **FTSL (failure to seek low)** = confluence. *"You're [failure] to seek. It's a confluence. You don't technically need it, but it is a confluence."*
5. **Retrace to the FVG at the previous structure** — *"why is it super important to retrace the previous structure? Is that usually the last point where we're gonna retrace? Yes."*
6. **Protected low = stop. Objective high = target.** *"This is your stop and this is your target."*
7. Optional: *"you could also have stop here and you do a 1-minute inversion [IFVG] if you need that extra little step."*
- **A+ requirement:** *"This is all you need if your alignment is bullish. Everything is bullish. This is all you need."*

### The Mistake to Avoid
- *"Here's the one mistake I made… no shift yet. Waiting for the displacement is super important — didn't shift above. That's it."* → **Never enter the reversal before price displaces/shifts above the structured high.** *"A reversal would be you have to displace above the structured high first, then retrace, then shoot for the high."*

### Continuation Model — anatomy (verbatim)
- *"From the high: impulsive displacement → shift → FVG → inversion FVG → fair value premium sequence (internal/external) → higher-timeframe FVG → multi-timeframe alignment → FTSL lows as confluence. That's it. Clean, effective."*
- **Continuation = all about R.** *"The continuation model is all about R."*
- **Previous structure is non-negotiable for the draw:** *"Point of interest comes from previous structure. Previous structure → model → model → two attempts."*
- **Structure before displacement:** when marking the previous structure, *"it's the structure before displacement."*

### Hard "No Trade" Rules (loud / emphatic)
- **The high must be clear:** *"If the high is not clear, I won't take the trade. I don't give a shit if you pump a thousand points. It needs to be clear as day, so I have somewhere to go break even and just trail it."*
- **No longing at the high (continuation must rebalance AT structure):** *"Especially at all-time highs… needs a rebalance at structure. It needs to be here or nowhere. If it's not here, I'm not longing. Don't ever ask me — I will literally ban you."*
- **No trades above all-time high** for him personally.
- **If you won't trade in manipulation, don't** — wait for price to reverse and trade continuation **the next day** (cleaner model).

### Numbers / Stats (verbatim)
- **Timing:** *"The most efficient screen time is two hours New York. Anything over two hours your probability falls 50%."* Model must form *"the first one to two hours in New York."*
- **R/win-rate math:** *"Just off this internal high, I'm shooting for 5R. You'll have to lose five trades in a row. You can have a 40 win rate. You'll be green every day, every week, every month."*
- **Risk asymmetry:** *"Right now my biggest loss is one R, no matter what. My biggest win could be 7R, 10R, 20R, 30R. Last month I made 30R just in the journal."*
- **Past vs present:** *"You can go journal my trades in 2022/2023, I had a 90 win rate / 80 win rate… but I made way less money"* (trimmed winners early, losses ate winnings).
- **Frequency:** *"Two attempts a day, that's all you need."* / *"You can take two trades a week… five to ten trades a month and four or five of them can be red — you can be disgustingly profitable."*
- **Stop sizing examples:** structured-low stop ~50 pts "perfectly fits"; rejected a ~100–102 pt stop as too far (*"unless you have a really good R"*); a 65 pt inversion stop with breakeven at 1.64R was *"actually fine."*
- **Tenure:** *"How long have you been trading this model? Since 2022."*

### Trade-management Rules
- **Trail loose, anchor on POI/structure:** trailed a 3R trade and closed 1.5R; *"if you're trailing you need to really focus on point of interest — your models will instantly be 20% better, success rate jumps 10%."*
- **Don't re-enter for a worse fill:** *"Why would I reenter and get a worse fill? Keep the first initial entry and just trail it."*
- **Stops and PTs are sacred:** *"I'm in, my stops in, my PTs in, that's it. Respect it."*
- **First attempts being BE/small green ≠ wrong bias:** *"Sometimes it just doesn't deliver. It's price, nothing's perfect."*

### The A+ Model Checklist (the closing 5 steps)

2. **Multi-timeframe alignment**
3. **Point of interest (POI)** (= previous structure)
4. **Model** (reversal or continuation)
