## A

### AMD (Accumulation / Manipulation / Distribution)
**Also called:** Power of Three
**What:** The three-phase pattern price follows within any time cycle.
- **Accumulation**: Sideways/quiet phase, building positions
- **Manipulation**: Sweep against the eventual direction (false move)
- **Distribution**: True directional move into objective
**Layer:** L2 (state) + L7 (time)
**Tradeable insight:** The "manipulation" is where Rev Models form (the sweep). The "distribution" is where Continuation Models run.

---

## B

### BOS (Break of Structure)
**What:** Price breaks above a previous swing high (bullish BOS) or below a previous swing low (bearish BOS). Signals potential trend continuation.
**Layer:** L3
**Distinction:** BOS = in-trend break (continuation signal). MSS = counter-trend break (reversal signal).

### BPR (Balanced Price Range)
**What:** When a bullish FVG overlaps with a bearish FVG (or vice versa), creating a "balanced" zone.
**Layer:** L5
**Tradeable insight:** Strong reaction zone — institutional indecision resolved here. Seen in [[Trade Example.png]].

### Buy-side Liquidity
**What:** Buy stops resting above swing highs (where shorts have stops, where breakout buyers will enter).
**Layer:** L4
**Connects to:** ERL (External Range Liquidity on the upside).

---

## C

### CISD (Change in State of Delivery)
**What:** A subtle HTF order flow shift that precedes a visible MSS on lower TF. Advance warning that trend is changing.
**Layer:** L2
**NEW from research** — Recommended addition to your validation system for distinguishing real MSS from fakeouts.

### CLS (Central Bank Liquidity Sweep)
**What:** Specialized concept from ICT's Private Mentorship covering central bank impact on liquidity. Beyond free curriculum.
**Layer:** Advanced / not in current strategy.
**Action:** Skip for now.

### Confluence
**What:** When multiple signals align at the same price level/time.
**Layer:** L8
**Rule:** Models alone are not enough — need 3+ confluences for A+ setups.

### Continuation Model
**What:** Korabi's model for trading in the direction of established order flow. Uses IRL retrace as entry, ERL as target.
**Layer:** L6
**When to use:** Strong trend, no order flow shift, HTF aligned.
**Entry trigger:** Price retraces into IRL (fair value) within strong order flow → enter on confirmation.

---

## D

### Discount Zone
**What:** Below the 50% fib retracement of a swing — the "cheap" side of the range.
**Layer:** L4
**Rule:** "Buy in discount, sell in premium."

### Displacement
**What:** A strong impulsive move away from a level. Sign of institutional aggression.
**Layer:** L5
**Validation:** Look for large-bodied candles with clear directional intent (not lots of wicks).

### Distribution Phase
**What:** Price drives impulsively into the draw/liquidity. This is the "trade" phase of order flow.
**Layer:** L2
**Trade action:** This is where you EXECUTE. Models fire here.

### DOL (Draw on Liquidity)
**Also called:** Draw, Objective
**What:** The dominant liquidity pool price is being pulled toward — usually an unswept high or low.
**Layer:** L4 (primary concept) + L10 (psychology)
**Zion's rule:** If DOL is taken before a model forms → NO TRADES. Critical.

---

## E

### Equal Highs / Equal Lows
**What:** Two or more price highs (or lows) that match exactly. Creates a horizontal level where stops accumulate.
**Layer:** L4
**Significance:** Liquidity magnet — institutions will likely sweep these.

### ERL (External Range Liquidity)
**What:** Liquidity OUTSIDE the dealing range. Above swing high = buy-side ERL. Below swing low = sell-side ERL. Per official ICT: this is old highs and old lows.
**Layer:** L4
**Role:** The PREMIUM target — where price is going.
**Marked on:** Daily / 4H / 1H charts (HTF for bias).

---

## F

### Fakeout (Spoofed MSS)
**What:** A false break of a level that traps retail traders, then reverses.
**Layer:** L2
**Validation:** From your notes — look for decisive displacement (not just wicks), volume aggression at break, HTF alignment. See [[SPOOFED MSS.png]].

### FTSH (Failure to Seek High)
**What:** Price approaches a previous high but fails to take it out. Suggests weakness in the move.
**Layer:** L2 (confluence)
**Trade signal:** Combined with a model, FTSH confirms order flow may flip lower.

### FTSL (Failure to Seek Low)
**What:** Price approaches a previous low but fails to take it out. Suggests weakness in downward move.
**Layer:** L2 (confluence)
**Used in:** Continuation Model (FTSH in bearish flow = bonus confirmation).

### FVG (Fair Value Gap)
**What:** A 3-candle pattern where there's a gap between candle 1's high and candle 3's low (bullish) or candle 1's low and candle 3's high (bearish). The middle candle "skips" the price zone.
**Layer:** L5
**Validation:** Per Zion — no fixed size, validity is determined by whether price reacts inside it on retest.
**Important:** FVGs are not entry signals alone — they're points of interest within a model.

---

## H

### HTF (Higher Timeframe)
**What:** Generally Daily, 4H, 1H. Used to establish bias and major liquidity targets.
**Layer:** L7 (time context)
**Rule:** Always check HTF before LTF entry.

### HTF FVG
**What:** A fair value gap on a higher timeframe that aligns with a lower timeframe model.
**Layer:** L5 + L8 (confluence)
**Significance:** Strong confluence — speeds up and validates LTF setups.

---

## I

### IFVG (Inverted Fair Value Gap)
**What:** A previously-violated FVG that now acts in the opposite role (a bullish FVG that was violated becomes resistance).
**Layer:** L5
**Used in:** IFVG Model — trade the retest of the inverted gap.

### IFVG Model
**What:** Korabi's model for trading FVG inefficiencies, often in combination with order flow shifts.
**Layer:** L6
**When to use:** When you see clear FVG inefficiency + structural confirmation.

### Imbalance
**What:** A price area with significant buy/sell pressure mismatch, creating a gap that needs to be filled.
**Layer:** L5
**Often equivalent to:** FVG (but can be larger or smaller).

### IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm)
**What:** ICT's theory of the algorithm that delivers price. Uses 20/40/60-day lookback ranges.
**Layer:** L1 (theory — explains WHY price behaves the way it does)
**Action:** Reference only, not directly tradeable.

### IRL (Internal Range Liquidity)
**What:** Liquidity INSIDE the dealing range. Per official ICT, this is specifically the FVGs within the range (NOT order blocks).
**Layer:** L4
**Role:** The FAIR VALUE entry zone — where price comes back to before pushing to next ERL.
**Marked on:** 15m / 5m charts (LTF for precision).

### ITH / ITL (Intermediate Term High / Low)
**What:** Multi-hour swing points. Larger than STH/STL but smaller than LTH/LTL.
**Layer:** L3
**Significance:** Protected ITHs/ITLs in trending order flow = high-confluence reversal zones.

---

## K

### Killzone
**What:** Specific time windows when institutional activity peaks.
- London KZ: 02:00 – 05:00 ET
- NY AM KZ: 07:00 – 10:00 ET
- NY PM KZ: 13:30 – 16:00 ET
**Layer:** L7
**Rule:** Most setups fire during killzones. Outside = elevated noise.

---

## L

### Liquidity
**What:** The volume of resting orders (stops, limits) at a price level. High liquidity zones = where price often reverses or accelerates.
**Layer:** L1 (foundation), L4 (mapping)
**The institutional view:** Institutions need liquidity to fill large orders. They seek it deliberately.

### Liquidity Sweep
**What:** Price moves through a level just enough to trigger stops/orders, then reverses.
**Layer:** L4
**Trade signal:** A sweep of ERL followed by reversal structure = Rev Model setup.

### LTH / LTL (Long Term High / Low)
**What:** Daily / weekly major swing points.
**Layer:** L3
**Significance:** Major structural references for HTF bias.

---

## M

### Macro Time (20-min algo windows)
**What:** Specific 20-minute windows when the ICT algorithm "runs" for liquidity:
- 09:50 – 10:10 ET (Silver Bullet window)
- 10:50 – 11:10 ET
- 11:50 – 12:10 ET (lunch macro)
- 13:10 – 13:50 ET
- 14:50 – 15:10 ET
- 15:15 – 15:45 ET
**Layer:** L7
**NEW from research** — Recommended test: do these windows improve your win rate?

### Manipulation
**What:** The second phase of AMD. Institutions create false moves to trap retail before the real distribution.
**Layer:** L2 + L7
**Trade insight:** This is where Rev setups form.

### MFD (Macro Fueled Data)
**What:** Korabi's model for trading high-impact news events. Uses news-generated highs/lows as liquidity pools to revisit.
**Layer:** L6
**When to use:** Red-folder USD news days (CPI, FOMC, NFP).
**Critical rule:** Trade AFTER news structure develops, NOT during the spike.

### MMXM (Market Maker eXternal Move)
**What:** Alternative ICT framework for the IRL → ERL transition.
**Layer:** L6
**Status:** Conceptually similar to your Continuation Model. Reference only — no need to add separately.

### MSS (Market Structure Shift)
**What:** A break of a significant support/resistance level that signals a change in trend direction.
**Layer:** L3
**Distinction:** Different from BOS — MSS is counter to current trend (reversal signal). BOS is in-trend (continuation signal).
**Validation:** Real MSS requires decisive displacement (large-body close), volume aggression, and HTF alignment. See your notes on Bookmap/LuxAlgo references.

---

## O

### OB (Order Block)
**What:** The last opposite-direction candle before an impulsive move that sweeps liquidity AND breaks structure. Marks an institutional entry/exit zone.
**Layer:** L5
**Validation rule (Zion):** Must have clear institutional footprint — momentum, liquidity sweep, structure break. False OBs lack these.
**Important:** Per official ICT, OBs are NOT IRL. They're separate delivery mechanisms.

### Order Block Mitigation
**What:** When price returns to an order block to "fill" remaining orders, creating a reversal zone.
**Layer:** L5 / L6
**Trade signal:** Mitigation + structure shift = potential entry.

### Order Flow
**What:** The sequence and volume of executed buy/sell orders. Determines trend direction.
**Layer:** L2
**Two phases:** Distribution (impulsive, trade-able) vs. Rebalancing (retracements, wait).

---

## P

### PD Array (Premium/Discount Array)
**What:** ICT term for higher-timeframe institutional reference levels (HTF FVG, OB, equal highs/lows).
**Layer:** L4 / L5
**Used as:** HTF context for LTF setups.

### POI (Point of Interest)
**What:** A specific price level where you expect a reaction — could be FVG, OB, liquidity level, etc.
**Layer:** L4 / L5 overlap
**Usage:** Mark POIs before the session, watch for price reaching them.

### Premium Zone
**What:** Above the 50% fib retracement of a swing — the "expensive" side.
**Layer:** L4
**Rule:** Sell in premium, buy in discount.

### Protected High / Low
**What:** Intermediate-term high/low that has held despite retests in clear order flow.
**Layer:** L3 (when validated) + L8 (used as confluence)
**Rule:** **CONFLUENCE, NOT MODEL.** Must be combined with a primary model (Rev/Continuation).

## Q

### Quarterly Theory
**What:** Time is fractal — divides recursively:
- Yearly = 4 quarters (3 months each)
- Monthly = 4 quarters (1 week each)
- Weekly = 4 quarters (1 day each)
- Daily = 4 quarters (6 hours each)
- Session = 4 quarters (90 min each)
- 90 min = 4 quarters (22.5 min each)
**Layer:** L7
**NEW from research** — **HIGH PRIORITY** for solving model selection: each quarter follows AMD pattern, telling you which model is appropriate when.

## R

### Rebalancing Phase
**What:** Price moves to fill FVGs / inefficiencies. Counter-impulse to the main move.
**Layer:** L2
**Trade action:** Setup phase, NOT execution. Wait for distribution to resume.

### Rev Model (Reversal Model)
**What:** Korabi's model for trading order flow reversals after ERL sweep + structure shift.
**Layer:** L6
**Win rate:** Claimed 85-90% when all conditions met (Korabi's mentor's 2-year track record).
**10-step checklist:** See [[(C) Model_Selection_Decision_Tree]].

## S

### Sell-side Liquidity
**What:** Sell stops resting below swing lows.
**Layer:** L4
**Connects to:** ERL (External Range Liquidity on the downside).

### Silver Bullet
**What:** ICT's time-based model. Three one-hour windows per day (London 3-4am, NY AM 10-11am, NY PM 2-3pm). Mark liquidity, wait for MSS, enter on FVG retrace.
**Layer:** L6 + L7
**NEW from research** — Could become a 5th model in your playbook. Win rate per research: 55-65% at 1:3 R:R.

### SMC (Smart Money Concepts)
**What:** Broader framework that includes ICT methods. Focuses on institutional behavior, liquidity hunts, order blocks, MSS.
**Layer:** Synonym for the overall approach (L1).

### SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence
**What:** When two correlated assets (NQ vs. ES) diverge — one makes a new high/low while the other doesn't.
**Layer:** L2 (confluence)
**Tradeable insight:** SMT identifies which phase you're in. Divergence = rebalancing/weakness. Alignment = distribution/strength.

### STH / STL (Short Term High / Low)
**What:** Lower timeframe swing points (1m-15m).
**Layer:** L3
**Significance:** Used for entry precision, not bias.

### Stop Hunt
**What:** Institutional manipulation that targets stop-loss zones to trigger them, then reverses.
**Layer:** L2
**Defensive insight:** Place stops where they're hard to hunt (beyond clear structure, not just below recent lows).

## T

### Tape Reading
**What:** Real-time reading of order flow via price action. Advanced skill — pattern recognition through screen time.
**Layer:** Meta-skill (cuts across L2-L6).
**Practice:** Backtest extensively, then replay live markets.
